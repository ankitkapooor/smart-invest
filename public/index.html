Filing-backed company valuation

What is a stock price betting on?

ValueLens reads a company’s SEC filings, rebuilds its cash flows, and shows three readings of today’s price: the growth it requires, what analysts expect, and what the fundamentals support.

or press / anywhere

How it works

  1. Read the filings

    Structured facts straight from SEC filings, each traced to its source. A line a company doesn’t report is labeled, never guessed.

  2. Forecast the business

    A measured near-term forecast, then growth, margins and returns set by rules tested on 137 companies’ history. R&D counts as investment.

  3. Price the risk

    Discount the cash flows at a required return built from today’s Treasury yield, a published equity premium and peer-based risk.

  4. Read the price three ways

    See what growth the price requires, what analysts expect, and what the fundamentals support, side by side.

Three readings of every price

Market-implied

What the price requires

The constant revenue growth over five years that makes today’s price add up. A quick test of how much optimism is already paid for.

Analyst consensus

How the market sees it

Analysts’ revenue estimates and the margins their earnings forecasts imply, net of buybacks. Perception, clearly labeled as such.

Fundamental

What history supports

Cash flows built from the company’s filings and from what comparable businesses have actually sustained. Every assumption is visible and editable.

Measured, not promised

Every rule is tested on point-in-time SEC data. Where the evidence is weak, the app says so.
